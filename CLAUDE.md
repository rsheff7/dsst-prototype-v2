# DSST — assistant instructions

**Read [COLLABORATION.md](./COLLABORATION.md) at the start of every session.** It is the
working agreement between Robert and Neil, who develop this repo in parallel with
separate AI assistants. Five rules; one page.

The two rules that bite most often here:

- **Rule 4 — same file, coordinate before finishing.** `src/lib/llm-client.ts` and
  `src/app/api/analyze/route.ts` are touched by both sides. Whoever finishes second
  pulls the other side in deliberately, reapplies on top, and records the
  reconciliation in the branch description.
- **Rule 3 — the description carries the reasoning.** Every branch and PR description
  answers what changed, why, and whether it changed what a teacher or student sees.

Anything that must survive six months belongs in the repo, not in a chat thread.
